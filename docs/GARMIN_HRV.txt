GARMIN HRV - THE "SEGMENTS" METHOD

Code: garmin_code/garmin_hrv.py.  Use it with --hrv-method segments.
The DEFAULT is the notebook method (see GARMIN_ASSESSMENTS.txt).
Method ID: garmin-local-windows-explicit-bbi-v4 (21 Oct 2026).
Raw beats only; Enhanced BBI is never read.

Extra fields this method gives: VLF, LF, HF, LF/HF, SD1, SD2, SD2/SD1,
ApEn, SampEn.

These describe watch beats. They are not ECG and not proof of clinical meaning.


1. WHICH BEATS ARE JOINED

Inside each 6-hour block, two beats form a pair only if:
- their rows are consecutive in the original file (same source and table)
- they are at most 5 s apart          (--max-pair-gap-seconds)
- the time step equals the next beat interval within 50 ms
                                      (--hrv-clock-tolerance-ms)
A missed beat, a removed row, a long gap or a gap marker breaks the chain.
The beat filters (--bbi-screens, --bbi-local-median-pct) are optional
and OFF by default, as for the notebook method.

Time axis = the real UTC time stamps. Gaps are never closed up.
Note: 86-92% of time steps match within 10 ms; the result barely changes
between 10 and 300 ms.


2. FREQUENCY (LF, HF, LF/HF, VLF)

- full 5-minute pieces (300 s), no overlap, only inside continuous beats
- cubic interpolation at 4 Hz, never across a break
- Welch, Hann window, linear detrend
- LF 0.04-0.15 Hz, HF 0.15-0.40 Hz, in ms2; LF/HF blank if HF = 0
- VLF 0.003-0.04 Hz: separate full 1-hour pieces; shorter = blank
Reference: HRV Task Force 1996.


3. POINCARE (SD1, SD2)

For each pair (x, next):
- SD1 = SD of (next - x) / sqrt(2)
- SD2 = SD of (next + x) / sqrt(2)
At least 2 pairs. SD2/SD1 blank if SD1 = 0.


4. ENTROPY (ApEn, SampEn)

- pieces of 1000 consecutive beats (--hrv-entropy-beats, 1000-10000)
- m = 2, r = 0.2 x SD, natural log
- no gap bridging, no subsampling
- SampEn with no matches = blank (with a reason)


5. FROM PIECES TO ONE BLOCK VALUE

- frequency and entropy: median of the pieces
- LF/HF: median of the piece ratios
- SD1/SD2: all accepted pairs of the block
- every value has its count, status and review flag


6. WHAT CHANGED FROM THE OLD CODE

- old: all beats of 6 h joined          now: only continuous pieces
- old: time made by adding intervals    now: real time stamps
- old: 64-s Welch, from 50 s of data    now: full 5-min pieces; 1 h for VLF
- old: rounded to 2 decimals            now: full precision
- old: 300 points spread over 6 h       now: 1000 consecutive beats
- old: ApEn formula wrong               now: corrected and tested
- old: SD2 from all beats               now: from pairs

Outputs: hrv_epochs.csv (each piece, with source rows and hashes),
hrv_blocks.csv (one row per block), method.json (all settings).
